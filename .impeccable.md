## Design Context

### Users
Matthew, the solo developer and team lead, reviews internal assessments across
PEMEDES, DTAP and later team systems. Reports must explain impact and next actions
without requiring specialist penetration-testing knowledge.

### Brand Personality
Clear, trustworthy, practical. Help the reader understand uncertainty, not feel
falsely reassured by a green score.

### Aesthetic Direction
User-selected (2026-10-01): mirror the CERT-PH penetration testing report structure
(cover, details table, executive summary, key findings with OWASP Top 10 mapping,
recommendation, 4.1/4.2/4.3 findings, clean-up, disclaimer, appendices) so reports
read like the external VAPT. Every page must still be unmistakably ours: MVK badge,
amber "NOT ISSUED BY CERT-PH" ribbon, diagonal "MATT VAPT KIT | SELF-ASSESSMENT"
watermark, `MVK-` report ID and MVK-SAR footer. Never use CERT-PH logos, signatures,
ticket numbers, analyst names or verbatim boilerplate. Navy tables, amber MVK accents.
Print-first A4, readable at laptop size.

### Design Principles
1. Lead with plain-language impact and what to fix.
2. Distinguish confirmed observations, suspected risk and untested coverage.
3. Evidence stays readable, captioned, redacted and searchable.
4. Use warning text as well as color; avoid dense card grids.
5. No third-party assets or requests when viewing sensitive reports.
